Capstone Proposal

This project explores the feeling of not knowing if you can eat something, through the lens of living with (severe) food allergies. This project is personal to me as I have multiple food allergies, which I've spent my whole life reading every label, inquiring about ingredients, and never being able to just eat without thinking about it first. I’ve noticed that most people around me struggle to understand how this experience feels, and I want to change that. My proposal is an interactive web game where users are onboarded with an allergen profile, either their real allergies or a randomly assigned set, and then presented with common foods they have to guess are safe or not, with almost no information to go on. After each guess, the user can drag a lens (div) that reveals the hidden allergens inside the food. A wrong guess will trigger an interactive error state, while a correct one continues the game. After, users can scroll into a data section that shows just how widespread, underreported, and poorly labeled food allergies are.

The lenses I will be drawing on are mostly mental models and data visualization, drawing on statistics from FARE and the CDC. I chose these because most individuals easily trust food labels, and I want them to feel what it’s like to scrutinize food labels for hidden allergens. I will also aim to incorporate some speculative design in this project, imagining the possibilities for transparent food labeling. For creating this project, I’m planning on using the GSAP library along with experimenting with Motion or Chart.js for the data section.

My biggest uncertainties right now are scope, as this topic could go in many directions, and I need to stay focused on a certain experience by narrowing it down. I am also still trying to figure out which libraries to use for which parts of the project. My primary audience is college students in their late teens and early twenties who often share meals with others, cook for friends, or know someone with food allergies but have never had to think so carefully about what is in their food. Mainly, this project is for someone who might know individuals who have food allergies but just don’t know the feeling of dealing with those allergies daily, and to help improve the need for allergen labeling on foods.
